Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the core system rules

Do I need additional exercise equipment?

No. The entire system is carefully designed so that you require zero extra equipment. You only need your standard controller (Xbox or PS-style) and the system guidelines.

Why does muscle memory matter so much?

If you are trying to learn a brand-new game while also learning the brand-new workout movements, your brain gets overwhelmed and the experience will feel clunky. We highly advise playing a game you already have strong muscle memory for. This allows your physical movements to pair naturally with your gameplay.

Will my in-game stats suffer?

Yes, temporarily during active exercising. Your focus is split. However, once you complete the workout phase, your circulation is hyper-stimulated and you will experience a dramatic increase in focus—straight into the gaming zone!
